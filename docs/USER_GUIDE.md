# User Guide

## Dashboard

Shows:

- monthly income
- monthly expenses
- monthly surplus
- months to goal
- asset progress
- expense breakdown

## Income

Add salary, freelance income, bonus income, refunds, or side hustle income.

## Expenses

Add any new expense category.

Examples:

- Rent
- Phone bill
- Groceries
- Travel
- Medical
- Car repair
- Wedding expenses
- Subscriptions

## Goals

Update current balances and targets.

## Allocator

Shows recommended monthly allocation based on your current surplus and goals.

## Google Sheets

Import your existing budget from Google Sheets as CSV or configure live Google Sheets sync.

## Tax & Registered Accounts Planner

The Tax Planner tab estimates Canadian tax and registered account planning. It supports:

- Province selection
- Multiple income sources
- T4 employment income
- Contractor income
- Incorporated contractor estimate mode
- Business/self-employed income
- CRA-confirmed TFSA, FHSA, and RRSP room inputs
- Current contribution tracking
- Estimated federal/provincial tax
- Estimated CPP/EI or self-employed CPP
- Estimated RRSP and FHSA tax savings
- Contribution-room warnings and planning suggestions

Important: The planner is for estimates and educational planning only. Always verify numbers with CRA My Account, your Notice of Assessment, and a qualified tax professional before making tax or contribution decisions.
